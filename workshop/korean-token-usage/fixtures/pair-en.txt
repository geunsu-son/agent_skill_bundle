Long-term jeonse housing is public housing supplied under the Special Act on Public Housing and is not converted to sale. Transfer, sublease, or brokerage of the leasehold is an illegal act under the Special Act on Public Housing and is subject to punishment in all cases.
Applications for long-term jeonse housing must in principle be submitted via internet or mobile application (https://www.i-sh.co.kr/app).
- However, in-person applications are conducted only for persons with severe disabilities, older adults, and others who have difficulty applying online.
- Required documents and notes for in-person applications: pages 36–37
The occupancy-recruitment notice date of this announcement is Monday, August 31, 2026. This is the date used to determine application eligibility.
As a rule, occupancy eligibility is assessed as of the occupancy-recruitment notice date. However, income, assets, homeownership, and other occupancy qualifications that this corporation confirms through the social security information system for the purpose of finalizing eligible occupants are deemed to have been calculated as of the occupancy-recruitment notice date. Due to the nature of the social security information system, data from before or after the notice date may be retrieved, and the retrieved amounts cannot be restated to the notice date or to the current date for explanation.
This announcement follows a one-household (all members of the household), one-housing application principle. If duplicate applications are made, all applications will be invalidated, so please apply with care.
A housing subscription savings account is valid only if it is in the applicant's own name as of the notice date. Incorrect entries, including a wrong bank name, cannot be corrected, and incorrect entries may result in exclusion from winners. The subscription account is verified through the Korea Real Estate Board, and extra points are determined based on the query result; changes after the business entity's query (revival of the account, additional deposits, etc.) are not recognized.
[All information such as the type of subscription account, name of the joining bank, number of deposits, subscription period, and deposited amount can be confirmed by issuing a subscription account rank (membership) confirmation from the ApplyHome website (www.applyhome.co.kr) or by visiting the bank where the account was opened.]
Combined subscription account records are recognized only as queried through the Korea Real Estate Board, so you must issue a subscription account rank (membership) confirmation and check whether it is recognized for this announcement and what records are recognized.
Applicants must thoroughly review the announcement so that they do not suffer disadvantages during the application process (failure to understand eligibility, missed notices due to incorrect personal information such as name, address, or contact details, entering extra/penalty points less favorably than the facts, disqualification for non-submission of documents, mistakes, etc.).
- After an application is submitted, no modifications are allowed, including cases where required items for extra/penalty points were entered too low.
- If personal information (name, address, contact details, etc.) changes after application, you must immediately notify this corporation, or after the application period ends, directly update the address and contact details on the corporation website (updates are possible until 00:00 on February 19, 2027, 14 days before the winner announcement). If disadvantages occur due to failure to notify—such as non-submission of documents, no response to requests for explanation, or inability to sign a contract—all responsibility lies with the applicant, and this corporation is not liable.
For Seoul REIT No. 3 long-term jeonse housing, contract renewal may be refused when the statutory mandatory rental period (20 years from the lease start date of the housing) expires, so you must check the first occupancy year of each complex that includes the lease start date.
Completion and registration (preservation and transfer of ownership) may be delayed, causing later contract/move-in timing or making jeonse loans unavailable, and jeonse loans using funds may be restricted depending on the deposit amount, so please apply considering your personal funding plan. Seoul Housing and Communities Corporation, as a public housing operator, cannot be involved in the completion schedule or loans.
Inquiries about applications: Seoul Housing and Communities Corporation call center 1600-3456 (lunch 12:00–13:00; no counseling on weekends and holidays)
- Counseling proceeds without specific materials on each applicant's information or situation, so use it for reference only. Remember that responsibility for the application lies with the applicant, and be sure to review the announcement so that you do not suffer disadvantages.
Long-term jeonse housing is public housing supplied to unhoused Seoul citizens for housing stability at a jeonse deposit of 80 percent or less of the surrounding market price and is not converted to sale. If occupancy eligibility is maintained, residents may live for up to 20 years.
